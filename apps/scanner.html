Scan from a phone

The scanner receiver is not running on this PC. Double-click Open Scanner Receiver.bat in the Survey Unit folder, keep its window open, then press Check again.

On the public site, Chrome or Edge may ask to let it reach devices on your network: choose Allow. Other browsers may not reach the receiver from the public site; open the Survey Unit folder's copy instead.

The phone and this PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. If Windows Firewall asks, allow it for the network this PC is on (office Wi-Fi is often set as Public; allowing only Private then blocks the phone).

Start the receiver

Start button not working? Run Set Up Scanner Button.bat once on this PC (Survey Unit folder). The browser then asks to open it: tick "Always allow" and choose Open.

Point the phone's camera at the code and tap the link it shows. The phone opens the scanner already connected to this PC; nothing to type.

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC. If the camera shows no link, open its QR scanner (Google Lens on Android).
